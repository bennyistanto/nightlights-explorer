Nyepi 2026: an island at under a tenth of its light

Nyepi is the Balinese day of silence, when by custom lights are kept off for a night. It is the one night of the year when a dimming is known in advance, which makes it a test of what a satellite can measure.

What this page can and cannot say. March is the wet season: the only clear nights near Nyepi came ten nights later, so they are the reference, and readings the product marks as poor quality had to be admitted to see the island at all. In 2026 Nyepi also fell two nights before Eid al-Fitr, and the reference nights a week after it, so neither is an ordinary night. The page says how much light was seen. It does not measure how many people took part, it says nothing about electricity, and it has not been checked against any record on the ground.
