Lombok 2018: two dark nights, one of them unexplained

Three large earthquakes, among many smaller ones, struck Lombok between 29 July and 19 August 2018. A satellite passed over every night at about 01:30. This page asks what it saw that nobody on the island could have seen, and what it could not see at all.

What this page can and cannot say. None of it has been checked against records of power or damage. The page compares light with the eleven nights before the main earthquake, with the same cells on reference nights seen from a similar angle, and with Bali. It does not say why a place was dim, and nothing here is a statement about electricity.

1 · What the island could not see

Most of the dimming was the angle of view

On the ground, a darker night after an earthquake looks like damage. On , three nights after the magnitude 6.9, Lombok's lit cells gave of their light on the reference nights. But Bali, farther from every one of these epicentres, gave of its own. Neither drop survives a fair comparison. That night the satellite looked almost straight down, degrees from overhead, and over these two islands in these weeks cells read dimmer from nearly overhead than from the side. (The product's guide reports the opposite over dense city centres, so this is a finding about these places, not a law.) What is left after the main earthquake is small: on the night itself, and on the two that followed, about a fifth down and close to the edge of the noise.

2 · What only a satellite could catch

Two dark nights, and only one has an earthquake

The third earthquake struck at 22:56 on 19 August. The satellite passed about two and a half hours later. Against nights seen from a similar angle, the cells of Lombok seen that night gave of their light; Bali gave . A second test agrees: sixteen nights earlier the satellite saw the island from nearly the same angle, and over the cells seen on both nights Lombok gave of what it gave then. The night after, it was back at . So about half the light of the cells seen was missing for one night. Only of the island's cells were seen, so this is not a statement about the whole island.

The darkest night of the month is a different one. On Lombok gave of its light at that angle, with of its cells seen, and it was back the next night. Bali was mostly under cloud, but the of its cells that were seen read : nothing unusual there. None of the three large earthquakes fell on that night. This page cannot say whether it was a power failure, something in the sky that the quality flag missed, or a fault of the product, and it has not been checked against any record. It is the most unusual thing the satellite saw this month, and nothing here explains it.

3 · Where the shaking was, and how little light is there

Near the epicentre there is almost nothing to watch

The obvious question is whether places nearer the epicentre dimmed more. Night lights cannot answer it here. Of Lombok's lit cells, only lie within 30 km of the epicentre of the main earthquake. Three nights after it the band 15 to 30 km out read against Bali, and the same cells, followed through the nights before the main earthquake, ranged from to . A handful of cells swings that much on its own. After the main earthquake the bands beyond 30 km, which hold nearly all the light, show no drop larger than the scatter. On 19 August every band is down by about half: that is the dark night of the section above, not something that depends on distance. Night lights watch the towns of the south and west, not the north of the island.

4 · What stayed invisible

Nothing was seen to go out for good, and nothing new lit up

Two things that a survey on the ground might report were not found in these nights. Of the lit cells on Lombok that could be judged, none went dark and stayed dark. And no dark cell within 40 km of the epicentre became lit: lighting too faint for this sensor, if there was any, leaves no trace. On the night of the main earthquake too few of Bali's cells were seen for the plain comparison; against nights seen alike Lombok reads about a fifth down, which is inside the spread of its quiet nights.
